Privacy policy

Finance is a private app used only by its owner.

Google data

  • The app uses the Google Calendar scope calendar.app.created. It can only see and change the one "Work" calendar it creates itself, not any other calendar.
  • It writes one event per worked day to that calendar. It does not read other calendars, contacts, email or any other Google data.
  • The access token is stored only on the owner's own server. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Access can be removed at any time with "Disconnect" in the app or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
